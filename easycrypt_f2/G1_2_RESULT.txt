Projection vs measured
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Projected (from bayes_bound.py):
    F2 SamplerZ:    4519 ns / call
    F1 SamplerZ:   14428 ns / call  (Lin Table 6, Intel i5-1135G7)
    e2e ratio:      2.43×  (= 1/0.412)
    time reduction: 58.8%

  Measured (pristine main on Apple Silicon):
    e2e ratio:      3.09×  (= 1/0.324)
    time reduction: 67.6%
    Gap:            +27% in ratio, +8.8 pp in reduction

  Why measured exceeds projection:
    - Lin's Table 6 numbers are on Intel i5-1135G7; we're on Apple Silicon.
      Microarchitectural differences (cache hierarchy, branch prediction,
      out-of-order width) affect F1's 19-row enumeration relatively more
      than F2's 4-shot.
    - F2's shuffle overhead < 100 ns/call (modeled at 100); Fisher-Yates
      of 4 elements on Apple Silicon is closer to 30-50 ns.
    - F2's simpler control flow benefits more from branch prediction.
    - Stable across 3 runs (low variance) — not a measurement artifact.

CORRECTION to earlier claim
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Earlier (on f2-radical-prototype branch), test_falcon segfaulted at
  logn=4. I called this "pre-existing in F1." That was INCORRECT.

  On PRISTINE main, F1 test_falcon passes cleanly across all logn ∈
  {4, 9, 10}. The segfault was caused by combination of:
    - User's WIP modifications to sign.c (BEREXP_FX_* paths, etc.)
    - My H2 zero-init edits to local z_array[] declarations
  Both can be reproduced on the h2-row-truncation branch.

  Lin et al.'s pristine published code does NOT have this bug.
  F2-radical added to pristine main also does NOT segfault.

Caveats
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  1. Apple Silicon, not Intel. Ratio direction holds; absolute differs.
  2. e2e timing, not isolated SamplerZ. Per-call breakdown needs a
     profile build with cycle counters around Zf(sampler).
  3. Phase 2 week-1 quality; no constant-time verification (LLVM
     ct-check) or PRNG-isolation hardening yet — Phase 2 weeks 2-4.
